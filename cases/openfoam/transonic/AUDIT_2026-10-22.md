## 3. Solver performance (audit, 2026-10-22)

- FD Newton–Krylov: ~2.5–5k residual evals/epoch, 81-step cap, \|F\|~1e-3.
- Frozen epoch operator is affine once `_nodal_rho` is frozen (verified
  2.7e-16); 36-colour FD Jacobian costs 37 residual evals.
- Sparse GMRES (Jacobi): g80 epoch **12 s vs ~60 s** (5×); residual
  1e-8-level solve instead of 1e-3.
- Remaining bottleneck: GMRES iteration count under 500–1200:1 grid
  anisotropy — the x-line Thomas preconditioner is the next lever.
- Campaign long test (52 × g80 ep8) drops from ~7 h to ~1.5 h; with the
  line preconditioner, to well under 1 h.
